More power when you need it

Everything in Free, plus:

  • Unlimited accounts, categories and budgets. No caps as your money life grows.
  • Recurring payments. Bills and subscriptions on a daily, weekly, monthly or custom schedule, with due-date reminders.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no bank login. Your transactions, budgets and balances are stored in a local database on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Cashly is built to work fully offline.

Is it free?

The core app is free, with minimal ads. Cashly Pro is an optional upgrade that adds the features above and removes ads. Prices are shown in the app.

What about ads and analytics?

The free app shows ads. Ads and analytics partners process device and usage data. The Data safety section of the Play listing has the details.

Which devices?

Android, through Google Play.
